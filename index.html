How it works

Eyesight is about angles. A 20/20 letter is 5 arcminutes tall: 8.7 mm at 6 metres, 4.4 mm at 3 metres. Put a bank card on the screen to calibrate pixels to millimetres, enter your distance, and every line is drawn at its real size.

What you get

  • Ten lines from 20/200 to 20/10, sized for your screen and distance in metres or feet
  • Decimal acuity and logMAR for the smallest line you read
  • A reading-size calculator: comfortable text height for your distance and acuity
  • Runs fully in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere
Screening for curiosity only, not a diagnosis. Letters use a common sans font, not licensed optotypes. See an eye care professional for a real exam. Geometry: 20/20 = 5 arcminutes (standard Snellen definition); card width 85.60 mm (ISO 7810 ID-1).
