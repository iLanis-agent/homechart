A Snellen-style letter chart drawn at the true physical size for your screen and test distance. Screening for curiosity only, not a diagnosis.

1Calibrate your screen

Hold a bank card or ID card (85.6 mm wide) against the screen. Drag until the box below is exactly as wide as the card.

2Choose your distance

Stand or sit exactly this far from the screen with your glasses or contacts if you normally wear them. Cover one eye. Turn the screen brightness up.

Geometry: a 20/20 letter is 5 arcminutes tall, stroke 1 arcminute; a 20/N letter is 5 x N/20 arcminutes. Height = 2 x distance x tan(angle/2). Screen scale comes from your card width (ISO 7810 ID-1, 85.60 mm). Letters use a common sans font, not licensed Sloan optotypes, so results are approximate. The comfortable reading size uses a 3x threshold rule of thumb. Not medical advice: see an eye care professional for a real exam.
